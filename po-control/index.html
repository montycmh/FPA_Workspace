Assumptions: the width of each bar (100%) = the vendor's Plan. The top bar is Actual (already invoiced/billed); the thin bar below is the total POs (Request + Change Order, in USD) — the idea is that by year-end that PO bar should reach the Plan. Bills are not added separately since they're assumed to already be reflected in Actual via the GL (to avoid double-counting). If a bar passes the Plan line, the excess is shown in red. Amounts in other currencies (e.g. EUR) are shown but not converted or included in the USD totals. The PO→Vendor match is automatic (by text) and can have errors — review it in the table below (Vendor column) and correct it if needed. Each PO's fiscal year is derived from its date (FY runs Feb–Jan), so new POs will keep classifying themselves into the right year automatically as they come in.
